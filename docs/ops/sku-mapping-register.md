## Notes for the admin — please confirm these specifically

1. **Promo SKUs are reused.** `syn08` sold as "Promo Raya Haji" and now
   publishes as "Payday Sale" — same SKU, successive campaigns. The
   canonical variants for these are named "Seasonal Promo" so they survive
   renames. **Implication: a reused promo SKU keeps one COGS unless you
   tell us a campaign changed the package contents.** If Payday Sale ships
   different contents than Raya Haji did, we need an effective-dated cost
   change on that variant.
2. **Adipocyde SG price move.** The store now publishes S$79/110 where
   history sold at S$98/147 (2/3 Kotak). Canonical reference prices were
   realigned to the current published prices. Historical revenue is
   unaffected (order totals are the revenue source). Also note Synovil SG
   3 Botol publishes at S$147, not the S$110 that was seeded — corrected.
3. **Cavernosil naming.** Both Cavernosil stores sell their top package
   as "Pakej 3" — but MY's is canonically "Pakej 3+1" and SG's is
   "Pakej 4" at the matching price points. The mapping follows the price;
   confirm whether the canonical names or the storefront names should
   change so they agree.
4. **Five canonical variants have no storefront presence** (Synovil MY
   `SYN-MY-PK1`, Synovil SG `SYN-SG-PK2`, `SYN-SG-PK6`, and the two COD
   variants `SYN-SG-PK1N-COD` / `SYN-SG-PK2N-COD` / `SYN-SG-PK6N-COD`).
   They were part of the July seed but nothing published maps to them. If
   they're retired packages, we'll mark them inactive.

## What happens next

- These mappings are already **live** (unit economics attribute 100% of
  the last 30 days' units). Confirming this register locks them in;
  corrections are one-line changes.
- After confirmation, the remaining input is **COGS per variant**
  (Catalog → Products → "Set cost", effective-dated, MYR for MY packages
  and SGD for SG). Once ≥90% of sold units carry a cost, the Command
  Centre contribution card and customer LTV light up.
- Every mapping is audited (`catalog.sku_mapped`) and marked
  "pending admin confirmation" in its `created_by`.
